## Admin — Batch tag multiple episodes faster
Right now tagging is one episode at a time — open a card, add guest(s), save.
For a guest who appeared across several episodes (or catching up on a backlog),
this is slow.

**Idea:** a faster flow for tagging many episodes in one sitting — e.g. a
checklist view where you pick a guest once and tick every episode they were on,
or a "recently tagged, tag similar" shortcut.

**Status:** parked — revisit after core tagging (multi-guest per episode, done
in v44) has been used for a while and we know what's actually annoying.
