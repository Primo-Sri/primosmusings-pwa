This episode gives you the kind of division-by-division forecast in the NFC that makes the whole season feel alive.Primo, Steve, and Mark break down the NFC from every angle, starting with the league's most vulnerable division and working through a loaded North and a dangerous West. The conversation goes way beyond surface-level predictions - it gets into quarterback play, coaching impact, schedule advantages, roster holes, injury concerns, and the little details that swing a division from contender to collapse.They break down specific wins, wild-card spots, and how the NFC playoff bracket could actually shake out if the season plays out the way they expect. There are also sharp calls on coach of the year, breakout quarterback candidates, and the one veteran coach-room bet that could decide everything when the pressure rises. If you want a smarter-than-average NFL preview with real opinions, real disagreement, and plenty of bold takes, this is essential listening. Perfect for fans who want more than hype and want to know who is actually built to make a run.
