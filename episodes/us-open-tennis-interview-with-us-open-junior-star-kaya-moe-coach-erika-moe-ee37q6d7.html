US Open Tennis: Interview with US Open Junior star Kaya Moe / Coach Erika Moe

Fri, 05 Sep 2025 00:50:03 GMT · 00:32:27

Kaya Moe, the top-ranked junior tennis player in New England and a top-five player nationally, and her mother and coach, Erika Moe, offer their unique perspectives on Kaya competing at the US Open Junior Girls doubles tournament. They discuss their distinctive path from indoor courts in Connecticut to participating in the renowned Junior doubles event. This is a must listen episode on the incredible journey and sacrifices they have made along the way.
