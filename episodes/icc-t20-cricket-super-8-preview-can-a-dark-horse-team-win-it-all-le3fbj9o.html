ICC T20 Cricket Super 8 Preview: Can a dark horse team win it all

Fri, 20 Feb 2026 08:11:32 GMT · 00:10:20

The cricket world is buzzing as the Super 8s of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup kick off with surprises, rivalries, and the potential for an underdog story in every match. Zimbabwe's astonishing turnaround—toppling top-tier teams like Australia and Sri Lanka—and Pakistan’s resurgence are rewriting expectations, making this tournament anything but predictable. Who will emerge as the dark horses, and can traditional powerhouses like India, South Africa, and England claim their spot in the finals?


In this episode, Sri Primo breaks down the tournament’s most compelling storylines.
