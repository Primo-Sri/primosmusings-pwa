<p>In episode two of this five-part series, Primo and Carlos Badiola dig into the escalating battle between the PGA Tour and LIV Golf. They explore how high-profile signings—including Cam Smith and Brooks Koepka—shifted perceptions of the rival tour, and how suspensions, player lawsuits and the PGA Tour’s countersuit raised the stakes. Carlos explains why the 2023 framework agreement was first and foremost a way to stop the legal fight, even as it left big questions about whether the tours might work together. The conversation also examines world-ranking points, the fallout for players and fans, Henrik Stenson’s lost Ryder Cup captaincy, and the alternate future that might have unfolded if LIV had won its legal fight.</p>
