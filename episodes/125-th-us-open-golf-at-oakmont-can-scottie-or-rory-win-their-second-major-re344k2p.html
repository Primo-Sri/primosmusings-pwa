125 th US Open Golf at Oakmont: Can Scottie or Rory win their second major?

Thu, 12 Jun 2025 00:42:18 GMT · 00:17:29

The world of Golf turns its attention to the historic and difficult Oakmont Country Club in Pennsylvania. Daunting greens, long yardage , bunkers await as the golfers will try to navigate "golf's toughest test". Can Scottie or Rory capture their second major of the year or can the field challenge them. Who are the dark horses that could emerge from the field? Dr Dan joins me in breaking down the course, the players for the championship that ends on Father's Day.
