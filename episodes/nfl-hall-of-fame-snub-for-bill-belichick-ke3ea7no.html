NFL Hall of fame snub for Bill Belichick

Wed, 28 Jan 2026 11:55:03 GMT · 00:02:55

In this episode, Sri Primo delivers a compelling NFL update, focusing on the surprising exclusion of Bill Belichick from the Hall of Fame. Despite his six Super Bowl wins with the Patriots and two as a defensive coordinator for the Giants, Belichick's omission has sparked widespread debate and criticism from players, coaches, and fans alike. The discussion delves into the potential need for a revamp in the Hall of Fame voting process and the broader implications for the NFL. Tune in to explore this controversial decision and its impact on the sport.
