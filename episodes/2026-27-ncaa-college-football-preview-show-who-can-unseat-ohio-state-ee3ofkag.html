Primo and Joshua Kean preview the 2026 college football season by breaking down the biggest takeaways from Week 1, including Oregon’s narrow win over Boise State, strong early performances from Georgia, Texas, Ohio State, and Notre Dame, and the growing debate over conference strength in the SEC and Big Ten.The conversation also examines Michigan’s controversial 13–12 victory over Western Michigan, the replay decision that sparked questions about transparency, and whether teams should invest millions in untested high school quarterbacks. Primo and Josh look ahead to marquee matchups such as Ohio State vs. Texas and Michigan vs. Oklahoma before making bold Heisman, playoff, and national championship predictions.
