2026 US Open Tennis: Seeds bite the dust while Zverev and Madison Keys escape

Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:08:45 GMT · 00:14:00

Day five of the US Open brought dramatic comebacks and major upsets. Coco Gauff and Naomi Osaka advanced after tough matches, while Madison Keys survived multiple match points. On the men’s side, Alex de Minaur, Felix Auger-Aliassime, and Lorenzo Musetti were eliminated, while Alexander Zverev survived another five-set battle.


The episode also celebrates Gael Monfils’ US Open farewell, highlighting his entertaining style and lasting impact on tennis. Primo previews the next day’s key matches, including Medvedev versus Rinderknech, Bublik versus Paul, Alcaraz versus Wu, and several important women’s matchups.
