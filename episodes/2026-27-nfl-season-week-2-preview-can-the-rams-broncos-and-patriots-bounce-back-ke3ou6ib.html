Week 2 of the NFL is loaded with pressure spots, quarterback questions, and bold predictions. Primo breaks down the biggest matchups, including Bills-Lions, Colts-Chiefs, Rams-Giants, and Bears-Vikings, before covering the rest of the slate, quick hits, and surprising picks.


This preview introduces a new format focused on the marquee games, key storylines, injury updates, and the teams facing early-season pressure. Primo also examines Caleb Williams and the Bears, the pressure on the Cowboys and Commanders, and whether early surprises like the Jets and Cardinals are built to last.
