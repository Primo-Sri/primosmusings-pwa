ICC T20 World Cup Preview

Wed, 29 May 2024 10:06:35 GMT · 00:19:20
Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts Open app

The ninth edition of the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup runs from June 1 to June 29, and the 2024 champions will be crowned at the Kensington Oval Stadium in Bridgetown, Barbados. Amit Duvedi joins me in picking the favorites and dark horses. Will India finally win this cup since its inaugural edition in 2007. Can West Indies and England be the first teams to win the cup for the Third time?
