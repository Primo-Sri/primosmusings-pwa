NFL Super Bowl Recap: Seahawks dominate Patriots to win their second Lombardi Trophy

Thu, 12 Feb 2026 04:14:48 GMT · 00:55:50
Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts Open app

The Seattle Seahawks pulled off one of the most surprising Super Bowl runs in recent memory—from a 60-to-1 preseason underdog to champions. Primo is joined by Matt Altshuler, a diehard Seahawks fan , shares behind-the-scenes insights, pivotal plays, and the unwavering belief that fueled their incredible journey. Matt discusses Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker's and a resilient defense performance that carried Seattle to glory.

Matt breaks down the Seahawks’ masterful defensive schemes, their exceptional special teams, head coach Mike Mcdonald and how Seattle relied on Sam Darnold who becomes a SB champion.

Whether you’re a Seahawks supporter or a football enthusiast seeking lessons in perseverance, this conversation reveals that sometimes, the most unexpected victories come from a combination of bold moves, leadership, and relentless passion.
