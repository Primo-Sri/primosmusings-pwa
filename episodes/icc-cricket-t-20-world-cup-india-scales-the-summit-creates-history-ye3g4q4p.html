ICC Cricket T 20 World Cup: India scales the summit, creates history

Sun, 08 Mar 2026 23:27:11 GMT · 00:34:20

India displayed total dominance against the Kiwis and hoisted the T20 World Cup. India becomes the first country to win on home soil and also win the cup 3 times. India also wins back to back T20 championships. Sanju Samson wins Player of the tournament , while Bumrah wins Man of the match. Amit joins Primo in breaking down the historic win .
