The 2026 edition of the FIFA World Cup will be kicking off on June 11 with Canada, USA and Mexico set to host the 23rd edition of the soccer extravaganza. The tournament is already full of firsts as it is the first to feature 48 teams spread across 12 Groups. Soccer expert Chris Rogers joins Primo in breaking down each and every group and predicting the Top 32 teams that will make it to next round.
