2024 Wimbledon Tennis: Carlos goes back-to-back

Sun, 14 Jul 2024 18:36:35 GMT · 00:18:13

Carlos Alcaraz reigns supreme as he overwhelms Novak Djokovic in a second straight Wimbledon Final. This is his second major of this year and 4th overall in his young career. Is this the passing of the torch or will Djokovic bounce back in future grand slams? Die-hard Djokovic fan Dr Dan Kessler joins me in analyzing the impressive win for Carlos.
