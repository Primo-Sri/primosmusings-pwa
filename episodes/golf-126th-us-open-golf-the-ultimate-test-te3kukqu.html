Golf: 126th US Open Golf: The ultimate test

Thu, 18 Jun 2026 00:45:50 GMT · 00:22:04
Listen on Spotify Apple Podcasts Open app

The 126th US Open Championship is set to start tomorrow at the Shinnecock Hills Golf Club in Southampton, NY. This course is set up to expose every weakness in a golfer’s game, from brutal winds to thick fescue and lightning-fast greens.
That makes this a true mental and strategic battle, where precision matters more than pure power.
With so many elite contenders and a history of low scores being rare here, the winner will likely be the player who controls flight, spin, and patience best. The course’s pressure, conditions, and unpredictability make this one of the most compelling tests in golf. Dr Dan Kessler joins Primo is discussing the elements, the course and who takes home the trophy on Father's Day.
